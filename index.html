TransLocNet: Cross-Modal Attention for Aerial-Ground Vehicle Localization with Contrastive Learning

A vehicle localization framework that aligns ground-level LiDAR geometry with overhead aerial context using bidirectional attention, likelihood maps, and contrastive learning.

Phu Pham1, Damon Conover2, Aniket Bera1

1 Purdue University   2 DEVCOM Army Research Laboratory

Paper arXiv BibTeX

ICRA 2026

Abstract

Using aerial imagery as a scalable global map for LiDAR localization

TransLocNet addresses the viewpoint and modality gap between overhead imagery and ground vehicle LiDAR by learning cross-modal spatial correspondences.

Problem

GNSS can fail, SLAM can drift, and HD maps are expensive to maintain. Aerial imagery is broadly available, but matching it to local LiDAR observations is difficult.

Approach

LiDAR is projected to BEV, encoded alongside aerial imagery, aligned using bidirectional cross-modal attention, and decoded into position and orientation likelihood maps.

Motivation

Why aerial-ground localization is hard

Viewpoint gap

Aerial imagery is top-down, while LiDAR observes local 3D structure from ground level.

Modality gap

Aerial RGB carries semantic context; LiDAR BEV carries sparse geometric cues.

Scale and rotation

The search region can be large, and the vehicle heading may be misaligned.

Real-world mismatch

Aerial images and LiDAR scans may be captured at different times, especially in KITTI.

Method

Cross-modal attention aligns aerial and BEV features

The model uses separate encoders for each modality, a bidirectional attention module for alignment, and a likelihood decoder for uncertainty-aware pose estimation.

1. LiDAR to BEV

Project local LiDAR into a bird's-eye-view image for comparison with overhead maps.

2. Feature extraction

Use ResNet-34 encoders with multi-scale refinement and Fourier BEV features.

3. Attention fusion

Aerial queries attend to BEV keys and values, and BEV queries attend back to aerial features.

4. Likelihood output

Predict location and heading probability maps, regularized by contrastive learning.

Results

Qualitative examples and benchmark comparisons

The heatmaps show where the model assigns high likelihood for vehicle pose. Quantitatively, TransLocNet improves both position and heading accuracy across CARLA and KITTI.
